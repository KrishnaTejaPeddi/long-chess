How to play

Tap a piece, then tap a dot to move it. Pieces move like normal chess, but the board is 8 wide and as long as you choose, so the armies start far apart.

  • Moves per turn – move several times in a row.
  • Slide limit – caps how far rooks, bishops and queens glide.
  • Pawn first run / speed – how far pawns can walk.
  • King stride – the king can step further.
  • Reinforcements – a free pawn joins each army every few rounds.
  • Fog of war – you only see enemies near your own pieces.
  • King race – you can also win by walking your king to the far end.

Castling and en passant are not included.
